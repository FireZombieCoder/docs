# Privacy Policy

We collect the minimal data needed to provide services, operate this site, and improve quality.

## Data We Collect
- **Contact data** (email, name, company)
- **Usage data** (pages visited, approximate geolocation, device type)
- **Support artifacts** (logs, docs) you choose to share
- **Account data** for portals (if you create one)

## How We Use Data
- Provide and secure our services
- Communicate about projects
- Improve features and reliability
- Comply with law

## Sharing
We don’t sell personal data. We share only with:
- Vendors under contract (hosting, email, analytics)
- As required by law or to enforce agreements
- With your consent

## Security
We employ encryption in transit, strict access controls, and least-privilege practices. No Internet service can be 100% secure, but we continuously improve safeguards.

## Retention
We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law.

## Your Choices
- Opt-out of non-essential analytics cookies
- Request access, correction, or deletion of your data: privacy@iknowkung.com

## Children
Our services are not directed to children under 13.

## Changes
Material changes will be posted with a new effective date.
